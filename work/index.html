Sable Budget

A budget you keep by hand: a big keypad, monthly envelopes, savings buckets, and a quiet sense of what's left.

On the App Store since August 2026 · iOS 16.4+

What it does

  • Monthly budget with a calm "what's left · per day" pace
  • Category envelopes that fill as you spend
  • Savings buckets for the things you're building toward
  • Bill and task reminders that actually repeat
  • A month in review: top categories and one gentle note

What it refuses to do

  • No bank or card connections, ever
  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud
  • No analytics, trackers, or ads; zero network calls
  • No subscription; backup export and import stay free
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected

How it's built

Logging spending by hand is the whole method: that small moment of attention is what makes people notice what they spend. Privacy is a hard rule, not a setting. Everything lives in an encrypted database on the phone, there's no backend at all, and the app works fully in airplane mode. Backups are passphrase-encrypted files you can restore on any phone.

  • Expo
  • React Native
  • TypeScript
  • Encrypted SQLite (SQLCipher)
  • Face ID lock
  • Zero network calls
