THE ARCHIVE

early specimens from when this site was a lab · kept for posterity

These were the first things built here: small toys and games made to see what the site could do. They still work, but they aren’t maintained. The real work is over here.

SPECIMEN 001

Tentacle Garden

A generative art toy. Click and drag to plant glowing tentacles; they grow, curl, and sway in the current. Auto-grow mode included.
SPECIMEN 002

Abyssal Sonar

Type or click and the deep echoes back. Every key has its own bearing and its own ping. Sometimes the sweep finds things you didn’t put there.
SPECIMEN 003

Feed the Squid

An arcade snack break. Eat glowing plankton, chain combos, dodge the jellyfish. Three stings and the deep reclaims you.
SPECIMEN 004

SLIMEBALL

Blob volleyball against a ladder of sixteen rivals, from a sleepy backyard puddle to the summit. Arrow keys or thumbs.
