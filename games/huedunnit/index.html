Huedunnit

      A prototype. No cover art is shown or stored here, only nine colours worked out from each cover. Game titles belong to their owners; this is an unofficial puzzle. Your record stays in this browser.

      How to play

      1. The bar shows the nine main colours of a video game’s cover. A wider block means more of that colour. The small grid beside it shows roughly where those colours sit.
      2. Name the game. You have six tries.
      3. Each miss or skip opens a clue: a sharper map, the year and platform, the genre, part of the title, and last a map sharper again.
      4. A miss also tells you whether the answer came out earlier or later than your guess.

      There is one puzzle a day. Use the arrows to play others.
