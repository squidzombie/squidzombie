      How to play

      1. The bar shows the nine main colours of a video game’s cover. A wider block means more of that colour.
      2. Name the game. You have six tries.
      3. Each miss or skip opens another clue: where the colours sit on the cover, then the year, platform, genre and the shape of the title.
      4. A miss also tells you whether the answer came out earlier or later than your guess.

      There is one puzzle a day. Use the arrows to play earlier ones.
